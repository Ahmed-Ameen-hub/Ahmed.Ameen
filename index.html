About

Short bio

I work at the intersection of Islamic/Ottoman architectural history, epigraphy, and evidence-based heritage studies. My recent work also engages heritage science through multi-analytical study of manuscript materials.

Research themes

1) Architecture

  • Mosque architecture (UAE, Ottoman world)
  • Urban/commercial buildings (bedestens) & built heritage
  • Comparative architectural history and typologies

2) Epigraphy & inscriptions

  • Ottoman inscriptions in Greece/Balkans
  • Color, visibility, and the visual identity of inscriptions
  • Multilingual inscriptions and public communication

3) Waqf & madrasas

  • Independent madrasas in Cairo (Ayyubid period)
  • Institutional/urban history and educational geography
  • Foundations, patronage, and documentary/inscriptional evidence

4) Heritage science (manuscripts)

  • Material and multi-analytical study of paper manuscripts
  • Conservation science and evidence-based preservation
  • Museum collections and Islamic–African manuscript heritage
